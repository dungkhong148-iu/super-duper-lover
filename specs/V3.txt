WHAT TO DO
 1. List (GoalsScreen.kt): two sections, each with a small title (titleMedium) above its cards:
      - "Đang thực hiện": goals with completedAt == null (order: nearest deadline first, no deadline last, then id).
      - "Đã hoàn thành": goals with completedAt != null (newest completion first). Title shows the count: "Đã hoàn thành (2)". Hidden when there are none.
    Card of a completed goal: name, a full progress bar in the "income" green (ChiLiTheme.colors.income), the saved / target line, and the line "Hoàn thành ngày dd/MM/yyyy"
    (formatGoalDate) with a Check icon; NO "Cần để dành ... /tháng" line. Tap opens the detail as today.
    Empty state when there are no goals at all (existing). When only completed goals exist, still show the "Đang thực hiện" title with a one-line muted hint
    "Chưa có mục tiêu đang thực hiện" and keep the "Thêm mục tiêu" button below the sections.
 2. GoalProgressContent (GoalComponents.kt): if goal.completedAt != null show the completion line described above instead of the per-month line (even when saved < target:
    a goal marked complete early shows "Hoàn thành ngày ..." and its real saved / target numbers, bar = real ratio, not forced to 100%). Keep the existing "Đã đạt mục tiêu" (done = saved >= target) wording
    for a goal that reached the target but is not completed (a reopened one): show it as today.
 3. Detail (GoalDetailScreen.kt): in the summary card show the completion date when completedAt != null. Action buttons:
      - not completed: "Đánh dấu hoàn thành" (OutlinedButton, min height 48dp) -> repo.completeGoal(goal.id) (no dialog needed; it is reversible by "Mở lại").
      - completed: "Mở lại" (OutlinedButton) -> repo.reopenGoal(goal.id).
    "Thêm khoản để dành" stays available for both states (a completed goal can still receive money). Keep "Sửa mục tiêu", "Lưu trữ mục tiêu" (with its confirm) and the contributions list.
    The per-month text never shows for a completed goal.
 4. Rules: colours from theme tokens only (no raw hex), single-line labels with OneLineText, touch targets >= 48dp, looks right at font scale 1.0 and 1.25, works in light and dark theme.
    Dates only through formatGoalDate.
